# WanderPick room

A bright postcard loft for local-adventure discovery. The product truth is architectural: a physical **LIVE PLACES API / NO CRAWL** sign states that WanderPick uses the live Google Places API and has no crawler or local places index. A panoramic map window carries route lines and pins over sky glass; its daybed is the dedicated downtime zone.

Sea-blue painted boards, pale sky plaster, afternoon sun, postcard racks, packed day bag, compass table, and trail shelf make the silhouette distinct from Home and Folio. Wayfinders share a compass-eared species. Atlas, the map fox, opens the digest. The coral JOB WALL is scoped only to WanderPick demo jobs. No location history, itineraries, credentials, or personal paths are included.
